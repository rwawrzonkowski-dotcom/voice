About V.O.I.C.E.

V.O.I.C.E. stands for Variable Output for Individualized Communication and Expression. It is a free AAC speech app for children who are new to AAC.

Who makes it

V.O.I.C.E. is built by Richard Wawrzonkowski, M.S., LBA, BCBA, a behavior analyst, for the children he works with.

It is being used in a pilot with his clients now. A public release will follow.

What it stands on

  • Free. No price and no ads.
  • Private by design. No accounts and no tracking. Everything stays on the iPad. Read the privacy page.
  • Grows with the learner. It starts with six words and reveals more in the same spots. Watch the board grow.

Help keep V.O.I.C.E. free

A gift of any size goes toward the costs of building and hosting V.O.I.C.E. Gifts are not tax-deductible. Sharing it with one family or one clinician helps just as much.

Please note

V.O.I.C.E. supports, but doesn’t replace, an individualized AAC assessment by a speech-language pathologist or team, and it is not speech-language therapy. Using this site or the app doesn’t make you a client.
