Privacy

The short version: V.O.I.C.E. (Variable Output for Individualized Communication and Expression) does not collect anything about you or your child. There is nowhere for it to go.

Last updated November 18, 2026.

The V.O.I.C.E. app

  • No accountsYou never sign up or log in.
  • No adsNone, anywhere.
  • No trackingV.O.I.C.E. does not watch what you do or report it to anyone.
  • Stays on the iPadWords, settings, photos and teaching data are saved on the iPad and nowhere else.

Photos

Photos you take for buttons or camera scenes stay on the iPad. When V.O.I.C.E. suggests words for a photo, that work happens on the iPad itself. The photo is not sent anywhere.

Speech

V.O.I.C.E. speaks with the voices built into the device. On an iPad, these voices run on the iPad itself.

On some other devices, V.O.I.C.E.’s voice list marks a few voices “Needs internet.” If an adult chooses one of those, the words spoken are sent to the company that provides that voice. Voices marked “On this device” send nothing.

Backups

A backup is one file that you choose to save. It holds a learner’s board, settings, photos and data. You decide where it goes and who gets a copy. The adult passcode is never part of a backup.

A backup file is not encrypted or password protected. Anyone who has the file can open it, so treat it like a paper record. Clinicians should store and share it the way their workplace handles client records.

Offline

After V.O.I.C.E. has been opened once, it works without the internet.

This website

  • No cookies.
  • No visitor counting or analytics.
  • No outside files. Fonts and pictures are part of the site, so your browser is not sent to other companies to fetch them.
  • The practice games save nothing. Everything resets when you leave the page.
  • The hidden words game remembers which words you found. That list is saved in your own browser and is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.
  • The board on the home page speaks only with voices that run on your own device. If your browser offers only internet voices, the board stays silent.
  • The examples on the Features page speak the same way and play their sounds only after you tap. Nothing you do in them is saved, with one exception: the passcode game in Figure 15 remembers which surprises you found, in your own browser only, like the hidden words. It is never sent anywhere, and its Start over button or clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

The site and the app are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical details such as your IP address when your browser asks for a page. I do not see or use those records.

Some links lead to other websites, such as a donation page. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children’s photos on this site

No photo or video of a child appears on this site without written consent from the family.

Not medical advice

V.O.I.C.E. supports, but doesn’t replace, an individualized AAC assessment by a speech-language pathologist or team. Nothing on this site is medical or clinical advice for your child, and using it doesn’t make you a client.
