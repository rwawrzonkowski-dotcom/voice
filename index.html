Variable Output for Individualized Communication and Expression

A free, private voice for children learning to talk with AAC

Every word keeps its own spot, teaching is built in, and nothing ever leaves the iPad. Made by a behavior analyst for the children he works with.

Why V.O.I.C.E.

Every word keeps its spot

New words appear in places saved for them, so a child’s hands learn the board like a keyboard.

Teaching built in

Prompts that fade on their own, data on every trial, and reports for parents and clinicians.

Stays on the iPad

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Photos and data are saved on the device and nowhere else.

See every feature in action

Sixteen small, working examples. Try the teaching tools, the tone wheel, the scene maker and more, right here on the page.

Stage 1: 6 buttons

Every learner sees it their way

Five looks, set for each child. The words never move, whichever look you choose. Tap one to try it on the board above.
