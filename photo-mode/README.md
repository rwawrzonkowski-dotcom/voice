# DIBS Photo Mode

A photo mode for making social posts and cover pictures. Dibs, Wacker, a plain pigeon and the Chicago critters (Chance, Hiram, Ivy, Monty and Sue) stand in a small 3D paper diorama as flat cutouts. You pose them, give them emotes, costumes and props to hold, move a 3D camera, set the lens and the light, add stickers and a frame, and take a PNG for the platform you pick.

It works offline. Nothing is loaded from other sites and nothing is uploaded.

## Open it

- On a computer: open `photo-mode/index.html` (double-click works, no server needed), or visit `/photo-mode/` on the site. It is not linked from the site menu.
- On a phone: visit `/photo-mode/` once, then use the browser's Add to Home Screen. After the first visit it works with no internet.

## Using it

**Editing works from the picture.** Tap Dibs (or anything) in the picture. A small paper bar pops up just above it with only that item's tools, and three tabs sit on its ring. Tap empty ground to put it all away. One finger on empty ground still turns the camera.

- **The bar**: a character has **Pose, Turn, Size, Wear, Emote, Hold, Copy, Delete**. A prop, symbol, your own picture or the L has **Turn, Size, Flip, Emote, Copy, Delete** (plus **Give** when it is near a character, one tap gives it to the nearest one). A speech bubble has **Words, Color, Tail, Turn, Size, Copy, Delete**. Each button opens one short sheet with only that control. The bar follows the item when the camera moves.
- **Handles on the ring**: the yellow tab lifts the item, the blue round tab turns it (drag around the ring) and the green tab resizes it (drag away to grow, toward it to shrink). They are 44 px.
- **Two fingers**: when an item is picked and the fingers start on it (or its ring), pinch sizes it and twist turns it. Fingers that start somewhere else move the camera. On a computer, hold **Alt** and drag to turn the picked item, or hold **Shift** and use the wheel to size it.
- **Near misses count**: a tap within about 20 px of a cutout picks it, and the ring of the picked item counts as the item.
- **Pose sheet**: one scrolling strip of poses (Stand, Wave, Shrug, Proud, Peek, Hop, Look left, Look right, Point). **Fine tune** has the sliders, looking and body color. While the sheet is open, small brass dots show on the head and the wings (the brad places). Drag a dot, or drag the wing or head itself, and the part swings about its joint within the slider's limits while the slider follows. A second tap on a picked character, or a long press, opens Pose. The tail has no pose value of its own, so it has no dot.
- **Size sheet**: size, height off the ground, Mount (when raised) and **Place and tilt** (left or right, closer or farther, tilt, flip).
- **Turn sheet**: the turn slider, **Face camera**, **Face ...** for each other character, **Snap**, **Line up** and **Face each other**.
- **Snap** (the Snap button next to Undo, or in the Turn sheet; off at first): positions land on a 0.25 grid and turns on 15 degrees. A faint paper grid shows on the floor only while you drag. **Line up** moves the picked item to the z of the nearest other character. **Face each other** turns the picked character and the nearest one toward each other (kept within 55 degrees of facing the camera, because a paper card cannot turn edge on).
- **Wear, Emote, Hold**: the same costumes, emotes (with their move) and holding as before, now in their own sheets. Emote **Fine tune** has the left or right and size sliders. Hold has the five places, a strip of what is held, **Let go**, **Take off** and Fine tune for size, turn and offset.
- **Undo and redo**: Undo and Redo buttons sit beside the shutter on a phone and at the top of the side panel on a computer (Ctrl or Cmd + Z, Shift + Ctrl + Z, Ctrl + Y). Up to 50 steps of the scene (items, backdrop, photo, light, look, stickers, frame). Camera moves are not steps. A drag, a slider change, a pose, an add, a copy or a delete is one step each. Opening or importing a scene starts a fresh history. Ctrl or Cmd + D copies the picked item and Delete removes it.
- **Which hand** (Save tab): Right-handed (the default) or Left-handed. Left-handed moves the shutter, Undo, Redo and Snap, and the Done button of the sheets to the left (on a computer the side panel goes to the left). It is kept on this device.

**The six tabs**

- **Add**: add a character, the L (eight line colors), a speech bubble, a prop or your own picture. **In the scene** lists everything; tap a row to pick it and its bar appears. On a phone the sheet steps away after you add something.
- **Set**: **Backdrops** (thirteen drawn Chicago sets; a set can suggest a light), **Your photo** (Take a picture or Choose a picture; size, left or right, up or down, distance; **Floor** Paper floor, Photo only or Hidden; **Flat on floor**) and **Background motion** (Off, Matte pan, Paper loop; speed, direction, Play or Pause; it stops while the tab is hidden, starts paused if your device asks for reduced motion, and the photo freezes the current frame; `App.setBackgroundTime(seconds)` sets the phase for skits).
- **Camera**: Orbit (drag to go around, scroll or pinch to move closer, right-drag or two fingers to slide) or Fly (drag to look, pad or W A S D, Q and E to move). Lens 14 to 200 mm, aperture f/1.4 to f/22 (blur follows the thin-lens formula), tap to focus, focus distance, camera tilt, exposure, thirds grid.
- **Light & look**: a small **Light | Look** switch. Light has time of day presets, sun direction and warmth, fill light, shadow edge and the spotlight. Look has Craft detail (Crafty is the default: corrugated cardboard edges, rough cut edges, a little curl, brass brads, tape, glue tabs and torn tops on the set; Clean is the flat paper look), filters (Warm, Cool, Newsprint, B&W, Faded film, Chicago night, Pop), brightness, contrast, color, vignette and paper grain.
- **Decorate**: a small **Stickers | Frame** switch. Stickers are speech and thought bubbles, text labels, badges, shapes, tape, all the app symbols and your own pictures (drag to move, drag the corner dot to resize and turn, or pinch and twist). Frame has the platform size (Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, X, profile pictures, free), 1x or 2x export, safe-area guides and paper frames.
- **Save**: the scene saves itself on this device. Keep named scenes, export a `.dibsscene.json` file, import one, open the last 12 photos again, and pick your hand.

**Where the old controls went.** The Scene tab's **Selected** block is now the bar and its sheets: Height, Mount, Left or right, Closer or farther, Tilt, Size and Flip are in Size (Flip is also on the bar for props), Turn is in Turn, bubble words, color and tail are Words, Color and Tail, Duplicate is Copy. The old Pose tab is Pose (poses and sliders), Emote, Hold and Wear. Backdrop, Your photo and Background motion moved from Scene to Set. Light and Look are one tab. Stickers and Frame are one tab.

**Take photo** makes the picture at full size (up to 4096 px on the long side) and opens a sheet with Download and Share. The bar, handles, dots and grid are only on the screen, never in the picture.

On a phone the picture keeps its size while you edit: the short sheets open over the bottom of the screen, and the picture only gets smaller as far as it must so everything stays in view. Tap the picture, Done, swipe the top of a sheet down, or press Escape to put a sheet away.

Keyboard: Delete removes the picked item, arrow keys nudge it (Shift for bigger steps), Ctrl or Cmd + D copies it. With nothing picked, arrow keys turn the camera and + and - move it closer or farther. The handles can be reached with Tab and moved with the arrow keys.

## Movie maker

`movie.html` turns photo mode scenes into short keyframed paper-diorama movies and saves a video. It opens with a 20 second showcase made with its own tools. Photo mode has a Movie maker link at the top right, and the movie maker has a Photo mode link.

- **Shots**: each shot is a photo mode scene. Add a blank starter, copy a shot, import a `.dibsscene.json`, or take the scene last saved in photo mode. Reorder by drag or buttons. A movie can be up to 60 sec.
- **Timeline**: scrub the playhead, tap a diamond key to edit its time, ease or value, drag it to move it. Set key records the picked character (position, turn, height, pose) or the camera at the playhead. Pose chips, emotes, bubbles, costumes, light and filter set their own keys.
- **Editing keys, rows and shots (phone first)**: the timeline list is taller on a phone (150 px). A tap on a diamond takes the key nearest the finger, and the key bar has Previous key and Next key buttons to walk along a row, so keys that sit almost on top of each other are still reachable. Tap a row name (Position, Pose...) or a group title (a character, Camera, Captions) to open a bar with **Delete this row** or **Delete all keys for Dibs** (one Undo step). When Quick commands, Auto camera or script captions made those keys, a second button also clears that source so they do not come back. **Tools, Shot, Clear all keys** empties the whole shot (keys, Quick commands, cues, Auto camera). The selected shot card has a round x to delete it (when there are 2 or more shots), and **Add shot** opens a chooser: Blank shot, Copy this shot, From a template (the Script sheet) and More choices (the Shot page). After picking a shot or building a script, if nobody is on stage at the start (characters that `enter left` or `enter right`), a short message says when they walk in.
- **Motion**: poses step at 12 frames each second (on twos) and the camera is smooth at 30. Emotes, bubbles and costumes pop in with a short overshoot. Camera moves: Dolly in, Slow orbit, Crane up or down, Rack focus, Hold.
- **Captions** draw in an L sign bar inside the safe area. **Sound** is your own file, off until you turn it on, and is mixed into the export.
- **Export**: every frame is drawn one at a time, then played into a recorder at an exact 30 frames each second (.webm, or .mp4 where the browser needs it). Fast, half size is for phones. Save poster makes a PNG.
- **Saving**: the movie saves itself in the browser (IndexedDB, store `movies`). Named copies, and `.dibsmovie.json` export and import, are on the Movie page.
- **Tools sheet**: the stage, transport (Play, time, Set key, Undo, Redo), timeline and shot strip are always showing. One **Tools** button opens the tools in the same style as the settings in the VOICE app: a dark top bar with the title and a **Done** button that never scrolls away, then grouped rows with a colored tile with a drawn icon, a short value ("3 commands", "35 mm f/2.8"; no arrow at the end of the row) (Make it fast: Quick. This shot: Shot, Characters, Camera, Look, Text. Movie: Sound, Movie). Tap a row to open its page, "‹ Tools" goes back, and Tools reopens at the page you used last. On a phone the sheet slides up over the dimmed stage (a tap on the stage, Done, Escape or the browser back button closes it; no slide with reduced motion). From 821 px wide it is docked beside the timeline and Done collapses it; from 1100 px the list sits on the left and the page on the right. Add a command, Script and Export have Close in their top bar.
- **Quick page** (Tools, Make it fast): short command lists for each character, so a busy parent can make a scene without keyframes.
  - **Commands**: pick a character, tap Add a command (Walk path, Wave, Say, Hop, Look, Wait, Wear, a dance move, a pose, an emote or a recipe). Commands run in order for each character, and characters run side by side. Drag the handle, or use Up and Down, to reorder. Tap a command to change it. Each row shows its time ("1.5 sec"). Say takes 2.5 sec plus 0.06 sec for each letter. The shot grows to fit its commands (the movie stops at 60 sec, with a plain warning).
  - **Dance moves** (Add a command, Dance): **Bounce** (knees bend and a small hop on every beat, a little sway), **Sway** (the wings swing up and down in turn on every beat and the head tilts with them; a character with no wings wobbles its head), **Step left / Step right** (a short side step out and back on alternate beats, still facing the camera, ending where it started) and **Spin** (a hop, one full turn on the spot with the paper back showing for a moment, and a small squash to finish). Each has a length in seconds (0.3 to 30) and a beat length (`beat`, 0.2 to 2 sec, 0.5 when not set, which is 120 beats a minute). The row's editor makes the move longer or shorter one beat at a time, and Step has Left and Right.
  - **Draw path**: tap Draw path (the sheet steps aside and a bar over the stage says "Draw on the floor with your finger" with large Done and Cancel; the height handle and selection ring are hidden), then drag on the floor. One stroke finishes the path and returns to Quick with the new Walk command open. Cancel, Escape or the back button leave without adding anything. The path shows as a dashed ribbon in the editor only (never in a picture or the video). It is simplified, then the character faces its direction of travel (limited, because a paper card cannot turn edge on), waddles on twos, and settles with a small squash. Speed is Slow, Normal or Fast. Camera follows is a switch on the command.
  - **Auto camera** (a switch on the Quick page): a wide shot of everyone, then a smooth ease and a gentle push in (about 10 to 14 percent closer) on whoever is talking, with focus on them. It holds half a second before the cut. A camera key you set by hand turns it off.
  - **Script**: type `Dibs: Hey Chicago!` (a line is spoken), `Dibs: shrug, question` (known pose and emote words, `wait 2`, `walk`, `wear blue cap`, `look left`, `hop`, `wave`), `Dibs (wave): Hi!` (wave, then say) and `Scene: Hot dog stand` or `--- L platform` (a new shot). Characters missing from the shot are added, facing the camera and spaced apart. Lines play in order, one at a time. Unknown words become a gentle note under the box. Build asks first if the shot already has commands, and Undo brings them back.
    More script words: plain short directions work inside the brackets and in a line that is only commands, split by commas, "and" or "then" (`Dibs (walks to Wacker and waves)`, `Wacker: hop, then exit right`); filler words like the, a, over and just are ignored, `slowly` and `quickly` set the speed, `runs` is a fast walk, `turns to Wacker` and `faces camera` are looks, and `holds food` (also hot dog, snack, pizza, drink, soda, or any picture word like `holds juice`; `holds nothing` empties the hand) puts a picture word in the right hand for the whole shot. `walk to Wacker` stops beside that character, `walk left`, `walk right`, `walk to middle`, `walk to front` and `walk back` move a short way, and `walk fast to Wacker` or `run to Wacker` set the speed. `enter left` / `enter right` (also `comes in from the right`) start the character off screen and walk in, and `exit left` / `exit right` (also `leaves left`, `goes off right`) walk off before the character goes; plain `enter` and `exit` still pop in and out. A line that starts with `&` starts at the same time as the line before it (`& Dibs: shrug`), so two characters can move together. `Camera: wide`, `close on Dibs`, `push in`, `pull out`, `orbit` and `auto` (also `Cam:`) and `Caption: words` / `Caption: off` are timed where they appear in the script. Camera lines turn Auto camera on, and are skipped, with a note, when the shot has camera keys you made; captions are skipped when the shot already has captions you made. `Scene: Hot dog stand, morning` (or `--- L platform at night`) also sets the light (morning, noon, golden hour, blue hour, night, studio), `Scene: same` keeps the place, and a shot that keeps the place of the shot before it starts with the characters where they ended. A brand new movie's untouched first shot is filled by the script instead of adding a shot.
  - **Dance script words**: `bounce 4`, `sway 4`, `step left 4`, `step right 2`, `spin 2` (the number is beats; defaults 4, 4, 2 and 2; `bounces`, `sways`, `steps left`, `spins`, `twirl` and `dance`, which is `bounce 4`, also work). They are commands, so `Dibs: bounce 4, spin 2` is two commands and not speech. `Tempo: 120` (or `BPM: 120`, 40 to 220, default 120) sets the beats a minute for the lines after it, also in later shots until it changes; it is not a timed line. Build turns beats into seconds (`beats * 60 / bpm`) and stores the beat length on the move. `Places: Dibs front, Chance back left, Ivy back right` puts characters where you say before the lines run (words: front, back, far back, left, right, far left, far right; a character with no side stands in the middle), facing the camera.
  - **Script templates**: at the top of the Script sheet, seven templates (Tip of the day, Two-critter chat, Wacker steals a snack, Big reveal, Walk and talk, TikTok dance, Word of the week) give a short form (words to type, and chips for the characters, picture word and place). **Put in my script** fills the box (it asks once before replacing words already there) and **Build it now** fills it and builds. TikTok dance has a leader, up to three more dancers (each can be Nobody), a Tempo and a caption, and runs bounce, sway, side steps and a spin together (about 16 sec at 120 beats a minute); add your song in Tools, Sound and set Tempo to its beats a minute. The templates are plain script text, so you can change them afterwards. They live in `js/movie-templates.js`.
  - **Recipes**: Save as recipe keeps one character's list (paths are saved relative to where it starts). Add a command, then Recipes, applies one to the picked character. Walk in and say hi, Big reveal and Wacker heckle come with the tool. Saved recipes live in IndexedDB (store `recipes`, database version 3).
  - **How it works**: commands live on the shot (`shot.actions`, `shot.autoCamera`, saved in `.dibsmovie.json`; older files without them still open). `MM.compileActions` rebuilds the keys they stand for on ordinary tracks, each marked `gen: true`, whenever a list changes. Keys you make by hand are never touched. Changing a generated key in the timeline makes it yours.
- **Files**: `movie.html`, `css/movie.css`, `js/movie.js` (data, timeline maths, playback, export), `js/movie-actions.js` (command lists, auto camera, script, recipes), `js/movie-ui.js` (screen), `js/edit.js` and `js/movie-edit.js` (editing on the stage), `js/movie-quick.js` (the Quick page, the draw bar and the Script sheet), `js/movie-templates.js` (the script templates), `js/movie-showcase.js` (the showcase).
- **Editing on the stage** (the same as photo mode, from `js/edit.js`): tap a character in the picture and the paper bar pops up above it with **Pose, Turn, Size, Wear, Emote, Hold, Copy, Delete** (a prop, picture or speech bubble in an imported shot gets the same bar as in photo mode; there is no Give). The ring has the turn tab (blue, drag around), the size tab (green) and the height tab (yellow). With a character picked, two fingers that start on it pinch to size and twist to turn; fingers that start elsewhere still move the camera. **Pose** shows brass dots on the head and wings: drag one (or the part) to swing it, or use the Pose chips or **Fine tune**. Tap the picked character again, or press and hold, for Pose. The first character of a shot is picked for you with its ring but no bar, so the picture stays clean until you touch it. Characters, Camera and the rest of Tools still work and are there for lists and exact numbers.
  - **Every edit goes through the movie's own model.** The first change of an edit pushes the movie's Undo step. When the edit ends, what changed is written as keys at the playhead, the way Set key does it: Turn writes `item:ID.rot`, a drag on the floor writes `.pos`, the height tab writes `.height`, a dragged or slid wing, head, lean, bend or hop writes `.pose.PART`. On a track that is new, a key at the start of the shot holds the value the shot had (so the change starts at the playhead and not at the start); a track that has keys gets a key at the playhead or updates the one that is there. Pose chips, Wear and Emote call the same functions as the Characters page, so they write their usual keys. Size, Flip and Hold are the same for the whole shot (they are saved in the shot's scene, not keyed). Copy adds a character to the shot, and Delete takes the character and all its keys and Quick commands out; Undo brings them back.
  - **Undo and Redo** are the movie's (`MM.pushUndo`, 40 steps), from the transport row, the sheet's header on a phone, or Ctrl or Cmd + Z. After Undo the same character is picked again with its bar and the same sheet open. Ctrl or Cmd + D copies the picked character.
  - **Snap** is in the transport row next to Undo and Redo (and in the Turn sheet): floor drags land on a 0.25 grid, turns on 15 degrees, with the faint paper grid while dragging. Turn also has Face camera, Face another character, Line up and Face each other.
  - **Short sheets**: a bar button opens one short sheet. On a phone it covers the bottom of the screen (up to 38 percent of the height) and the picture above it keeps its size, with Undo, Redo and Done in its header; swipe the header down to put it away. On a computer it sits in the side column above Tools. Opening Tools puts the short sheet away on a phone, and the other way round.
  - **Which hand**: Tools, Movie, Which hand (the same saved setting as photo mode, `dibs-photo-hand`). Left-handed mirrors the transport row on a phone (Tools, Snap, Undo, Redo, Set key, time, Play) and puts the side column on the left on a computer; the short sheet's header mirrors too.

## How it is built

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step at runtime. three.js (MIT) is bundled in `vendor/`.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.html`, `css/photo.css` | The page and styles |
| `js/assets.js` | **Generated.** The Dibs, Wacker, critter, costume, L and symbol drawings as SVG text. Run `node photo-mode/tools/build-assets.js` from the repo root after changing any drawing in `img/dibs`, `img/wacker`, `img/symbols` or `photo-mode/art`. |
| `js/puppets.js` | Paper cutouts (die-cut edge, cardstock back, stand), the puppets (each drawing is cut into parts by its own SVG elements), anchors for held and worn items, body moves |
| `js/extras.js` | Emote cutouts, costumes, the L list and the 3D speech bubble drawing |
| `js/craft.js` | Papercraft detail: cardboard flute texture, edge walls from the cutout outline, rough edge, curl, brads, tape, torn tops |
| `js/sets.js` | The backdrops, drawn as layers of flat paper at different depths |
| `js/dof.js` | Depth of field (thin-lens blur), filters, vignette and grain in one pass |
| `js/controls.js` | Orbit and fly camera and touch gestures (one finger, pinch, and the hooks for item pinch and twist, grabbing a part, long press) |
| `js/stickers.js` | Stickers and paper frames, drawn in 2D over the picture |
| `js/store.js` | Saving in the browser (IndexedDB: scenes, photo roll, autosave, movies, recipes) |
| `js/app.js` | The scene, capture and saving. Picking with slop, the ring and its height tab, `App.onHandles`, `App.commit`, `App.dock` (room taken by a phone sheet) |
| `js/edit.js` | Editing on the picture (`DP.edit.init(cfg)`, called by `ui.js` with its own undo history and by `movie-edit.js` with the movie's undo): the contextual bar (`App.edit.actionsFor`, `onAction`, `setActive`), turn and size tabs, pinch and twist, pose dots and part dragging, near-miss picking, snapping and the grid, face and line up, and the undo history (`App.commit`, `App.edit.undo`, `redo`). It switches on only when the page has `#ctxBar`, `#trn` and `#siz`, so `movie.html` is not touched until it adopts them |
| `js/ui.js` | The tab panel, the short sheets, undo buttons and left or right hand |
| `js/movie-edit.js` | Movie Maker: wires `edit.js` into the movie (undo step before an edit, keys at the playhead after it, the short sheets, Snap, which hand) |
| `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest` | Offline cache and install on a phone. Raise `CACHE` in `sw.js` whenever a file changes (now `dibs-photo-mode-v11`). |

Why the drawings are stored as text: browsers block textures loaded from image files when a page is opened straight from disk. Text works everywhere.

Dibs, Wacker, the critters, costumes, the L and the symbols keep the licenses in `NOTICE.md`; the drawing rules for `art/` are in `art/README.txt`. In photo mode only, Dibs wears a Chicago flag neck band (white, two light blue stripes, red six-pointed stars) and the Pigeon a solid black one; the drawings in `img/dibs` are unchanged. Anything you add stays on your device.
