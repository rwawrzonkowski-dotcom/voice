Drawing rules
- Every drawing is 100 x 100. Outlines #3d3a4f, 3 wide, round joins and caps (Wacker uses #2a2350).
- Parts marked class="body" take a chosen body color in the app; Sue and the L keep their own colors.
- Use the drawings as they are. Do not redraw or change the characters.
- No team logos, no CTA logo, no Bean. The L is a plain train with no face.
- Wacker is a smug rival, never a threat: he steals food, never Dibs, and is proven wrong. Nothing scary.
